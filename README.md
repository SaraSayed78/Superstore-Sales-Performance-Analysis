# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview
An end-to-end retail analytics and business intelligence solution constructed in Advanced Excel. The primary objective is to evaluate overall business performance, identify profitability bottlenecks across regional territories and product categories, and deliver an interactive dashboard with automated reporting features for executive decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Advanced Excel:** Data Modeling, Pivot Tables, Slicers, Timeline Filters, and Advanced Formulas (VLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH).
* **Business Intelligence & Visualization:** Interactive Dashboard featuring 12 customized charts and KPI cards, enhanced with in-cell Sparklines for micro-analytics.
* **Automation & Simulation:** Goal Seek analysis for scenario forecasting and custom VBA Macro automation for seamless dashboard navigation.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Business Insights
* **Overall Financial Performance:** Achieved a total revenue of **$2,297,201**, a net profit of **$286,397**, across **5,009 orders** with an overall profit margin of **12.5%**.
* **Category Drivers:** *Technology* emerged as the highest revenue and profit driver ($836,154 Sales, $145,455 Profit), with the *Canon Imageclass 2200 Advanced Copier* leading individual product sales ($61,600). *Office Supplies* also maintains healthy margins with $122,491 in total profit.
* **Profitability Bottlenecks:** Uncovered critical loss-making areas requiring strategic pricing adjustments, such as *3D Printers* (exceeding -$8,880 in losses) and the *Tables* sub-category (incurring -$17,725 in net losses).
* **Logistics & Fulfillment:** *Standard Class* dominates fulfillment channels at 59.77%, whereas *Same Day* shipping provides the fastest transit time but accounts for 5.27% of orders.

## ⚙️ Advanced Analytics & Automation Highlights
* **Sales Trend Forecasting:** Integrated linear trendlines with forward forecasting on historical revenue data to project future quarterly trajectories.
* **Goal Seek Scenario Modeling:** Utilized Excel's Goal Seek tool to simulate target scenarios, determining that **$3,208,414** in sales is required to reach a target profit of **$400,000** while maintaining a 12.5% profit margin.
* **VBA Macro Automation:** Programmed a custom VBA macro (`Reset_All_Filters`) linked to an interactive dashboard button to instantly clear all active slicers and timeline selections simultaneously.
